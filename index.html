How to work through it

Memorising a definition is not the same as owning a word. Aim to produce it: having learnt resolute, you should be able to write her resolution to become a designer led to her success. That is the level the writing band descriptors reward.

One letter at a time beats grazing across the whole list. Read a letter, mark what you know, then run the quiz restricted to what you haven't learned. Finish a letter well before starting the next — a hundred words you can use outrank five hundred you half-recognise.

Use the spelling test inside each card once the meaning is secure. Recognising a word on a page is a lower bar than producing it from the meaning alone, and spelling is where that gap shows up first.
